# Contributing

This repo is a method, not a codebase, so what's useful here is different from a normal pull request.

## What helps most

**A section from your own manual, with the counterparty anonymized.** The field guide teaches through contrast, and the examples get better the more of them come from documents people actually wrote and handed to a real team. Keep the weak version as something a capable person would genuinely write, never a strawman.

**A failure mode we haven't named.** The four in the field guide are there because each one is a specific, recurring move with a clear cost. If you can name a fifth that precisely, open an issue with two examples.

**Evidence about the install.** The claim that a manual changes AI output is testable, and the test is in `install-in-your-ai.md`. If you ran it and the second output moved on none of your three standards, that's the most useful thing you could send. A method that only collects its successes isn't a method.

**A place this breaks.** Contexts where the guidance is wrong, cultures where writing your own operating manual reads as self-important, org shapes where the document creates more rigidity than clarity. Those are more useful than agreement.

## What to skip

Added sections, reworded principles, and expansions. The spine is eight sections on purpose, and the guide is short on purpose. A pull request that adds length has to retire something to pay for it.

Also skip manual-writing services, templates with tracking attached, and anything that turns the exercise into a product. The whole point is that the articulation is the work.

## How to open something

Issues are the front door. A short issue beats a large pull request, because the discussion about whether a change belongs is usually the whole work.

If you're adapting this for your own company, you don't need permission beyond the credit the license asks for, and you don't need to contribute anything back. Reword it in your own voice and install it wherever you keep your standards. That's the intended use.
